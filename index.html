What to do with unknown & suspicious tokens

  • Don’t click links in token names, symbols or the airdrop message that came with them.
  • Don’t swap, sell or “claim” tokens you don’t recognise — that is how drainer scams work.
  • Don’t approve any wallet pop-up or spending permission tied to them.
  • The safest move is to ignore them. You can hide them in your wallet; you don’t need to do anything else.

This is a heuristic scan, not a guarantee. A token marked “Known” can still carry risk, and a “Suspicious” label can occasionally be a false alarm. Always do your own research.

How it works

  • Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking, nothing is stored.
  • Solana: Jupiter holdings API (token balances; public Solana RPC as fallback) + Jupiter verified list + DexScreener (liquidity, age).
  • EVM: Blockscout (balances) + DexScreener, Ethereum and Base only. Other EVM chains are not supported yet.
  • Token logos and links from token data are never loaded or opened, since those are what scammers abuse.
